# Upwork Proposals & Outbound Kit

Core principle: buyers on Upwork don't hire "developers", they hire **proof**.
Your unfair advantage is that you can say *"already built — here's the live
demo"* instead of promising a timeline. Every template below leans on that.

Set your storefront link (`STORE`), then paste per job.

## 6. Proposal hygiene (what wins jobs)

- First line references **their** words, never "Dear Sir"
- One demo link max — make them click ONE thing
- Price as two options when possible (license+customize vs custom) — anchors value
- End with a small ask ("15 minutes?", "send demo login?") not "hope to hear back"
- Reply within 1 hour of job posting when possible; speed beats polish on Upwork
- After delivery, ask for the review while goodwill is hot — profile strength compounds
